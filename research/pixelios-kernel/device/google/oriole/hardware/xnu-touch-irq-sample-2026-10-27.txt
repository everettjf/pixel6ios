Interpretation of the first run: the controller answers over SPI and acknowledges scanning,
but this run produced no finger event. The user later confirmed that they did
not touch the screen during this first 60-second window. It is a no-touch
baseline, not evidence of a failed physical touch path. The sampled GPIO level remained
high after one initial low sample. This is consistent with no sustained IRQ
assertion, but does not prove there were no short pulses: sampling is about
109 Hz and the behavior of PIN_DAT with this mux state is not independently
verified. The result alone cannot distinguish a sensor that never generated
touch events from pulses missed by polling or an event-FIFO acquisition bug.
