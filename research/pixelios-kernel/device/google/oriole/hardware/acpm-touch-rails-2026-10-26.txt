Observed: both bounded FUNC_READ requests completed with replies, zero return codes,
and no retry. No PMIC UPDATE request was sent. The phone automatically returned to

Read from source: for these S2MPG10 LDO controls, bit 7 is the enable bit (see
docs/research/23-acpm-regulator-client.md). Both observed values have bit 7 clear.
Thus the touch rails were disabled at this ABL/shim handoff. The lower VSEL fields
were 0x2C and 0x3C. The successful read does not establish the voltage at the
touch IC, chip identity, or that enabling the rails is sufficient for SPI touch.
